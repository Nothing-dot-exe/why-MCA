## 🟢 LOW RISK — Hard to Replace

| Job | Why Safe |
|---|---|
| AI/ML Engineer | Builds AI itself |
| Cybersecurity Expert | Adversarial thinking needed |
| Surgeon | Physical precision + ethics |
| Therapist | Human empathy critical |
| CEO / C-Suite | Strategic leadership |
| Plumber / Electrician | Physical dexterity |
| Social Worker | Human connection |
| Teacher (creative) | Inspiration + mentorship |

---

> 📌 **Key Insight:** Jobs involving **repetition, pattern recognition, and rule-based decisions** are most at risk. Jobs requiring **creativity, emotional intelligence, and physical dexterity** are safest.
