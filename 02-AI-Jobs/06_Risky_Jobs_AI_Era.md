## 🔴 CRITICAL DANGER — Gone in 1–3 Years

### Data Entry Clerk
- **Risk:** 🔴 98%
- **Why:** OCR AI reads documents better than humans, 24/7
- **AI Tool:** UiPath, Microsoft Power Automate
- **Real Impact:** 8M jobs already lost globally

### Call Center Agent
- **Risk:** 🔴 90%
- **Why:** AI handles calls 24/7, speaks 100+ languages, never tired
- **Real Case:** Klarna replaced 700 agents → saved $40M/year
- **AI Tool:** Intercom Fin, Zendesk AI

### Bank Teller
- **Risk:** 🔴 97%
- **Why:** Mobile banking + ATMs + AI fraud detection
- **Real Case:** Wells Fargo closed 800+ branches
- **Status:** Already 50% reduced since 2010

### Retail Cashier
- **Risk:** 🔴 93%
- **Why:** Amazon Go stores — zero cashiers, computer vision checkout
- **Real Example:** Amazon Go (50+ stores), Walmart self-checkout

### Paralegal
- **Risk:** 🔴 90%
- **Why:** Harvey AI reads 10,000 legal docs in minutes
- **Cost:** $50/month AI vs $70,000/year human
- **AI Tool:** Harvey AI, CoCounsel, LexisNexis AI

### Content Writer (Basic/SEO)
- **Risk:** 🔴 85%
- **Real Cases:** Sports Illustrated used AI secretly; Buzzfeed shut content division; CNET published 77 AI articles
- **AI Tool:** ChatGPT, Jasper AI, Copy.ai

### Bookkeeper
- **Risk:** 🔴 92%
- **Why:** QuickBooks AI auto-categorizes all transactions
- **AI Tool:** QuickBooks AI, Xero, Botkeeper

### Telemarketer
- **Risk:** 🔴 99% — Highest risk job on Earth
- **Why:** AI calls are cheaper, never tired, never rude

## 🟠 HIGH DANGER — 3–5 Years

### Junior Software Developer
- **Risk:** 🟠 83%
- **Warning:** Entry-level coding jobs DOWN 35% (2023–2026)
- **Why:** GitHub Copilot writes 46% of code; Devin AI completes full projects
- **Companies Cutting:** Salesforce (hired 0 new engineers 2024), Google (-40% new grads)

### Truck / Taxi / Delivery Driver
- **Risk:** 🟠 75%
- **Global Jobs:** 70 Million worldwide
- **Status Now:** Waymo doing 100,000+ rides/week; Tesla FSD 7B miles driven
- **Timeline:** Highway trucking 3–5 yrs; urban robotaxis 5–7 yrs

### Radiologist (Routine Screening)
- **Risk:** 🟠 85% for screening tasks
- **Why:** Google AI detects cancer at 94.5% accuracy vs 88% human
- **AI Tool:** Google Health AI, DeepMind

### Basic Graphic Designer
- **Risk:** 🟠 82%
- **Why:** Midjourney generates stunning art in 10 seconds
- **Cost:** $20/month AI vs $50,000/year designer
- **AI Tool:** Midjourney, Canva AI, Adobe Firefly

### Routine Journalist / Reporter
- **Risk:** 🟠 70%
- **Real Cases:** BuzzFeed shut news division; VICE bankrupt; Sports Illustrated mass layoffs
- **Status:** AP, Washington Post, Reuters all using AI for routine reporting

### Factory Assembly Worker
- **Risk:** 🟠 74%
- **Real Examples:** Foxconn replaced 60,000 workers with robots; Tesla Gigafactory 95% robot-operated
