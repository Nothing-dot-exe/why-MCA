# 1. 📐 Mathematical Foundations for Computer Applications (`22MCA11` / `MMC102`)

### 📺 Top YouTube Channels & Playlists:
1. **Neso Academy — Discrete Mathematics Playlist**:
   * *Search*: `Neso Academy Discrete Mathematics`
   * *What to watch here*: Set Theory, Inclusion-Exclusion Principle, Relations (Reflexive, Symmetric, Transitive, Equivalence classes), POSETs, Graph Theory, Handshaking Lemma, Trees.
   * *Why*: Crystal-clear whiteboard animations and English explanations.
2. **Dr. Gajendra Purohit (GP Sir)**:
   * *Search*: `Gajendra Purohit Matrices` / `Gajendra Purohit Probability & Statistics`
   * *What to watch here*:
     * Eigenvalues & Eigenvectors ($2 \times 2$ and $3 \times 3$ matrices)
     * Gauss Elimination & Rank of Matrix
     * Cayley-Hamilton Theorem
     * Bayes' Theorem & Conditional Probability
     * Hypothesis Testing ($Z$-Test, $t$-Test, $\chi^2$ Chi-Square Test)
   * *Why*: Explains the exact exam-pattern step-by-step numericals in Hindi/English that VTU professors directly copy into exam papers.
3. **StatQuest with Josh Starmer**:
   * *Search*: `StatQuest Bayes Theorem` / `StatQuest Normal Distribution`
   * *Why*: Makes probability distributions and statistical inference visually intuitive in 10-minute videos.

### 🌐 Free Websites & Problem Solvers:
* **Symbolab & WolframAlpha**: Free online step-by-step matrix solvers to verify your Eigenvalue and Gauss elimination homework.
* **GeeksforGeeks Discrete Mathematics**: Quick revision cheat sheets for formulas and definitions.

# 5. ⚡ Design and Analysis of Algorithms (`22MCA15` / `MMC203`)

### 📺 Top YouTube Channels & Playlists:
1. **Abdul Bari — Algorithms Playlist** (The Gold Standard):
   * *Search*: `Abdul Bari Master Theorem` / `Abdul Bari 0/1 Knapsack Dynamic Programming`
   * *What to watch here*:

     * **Divide and Conquer**: Merge Sort and Quick Sort recurrence trees.
     * **Greedy Method**: Fractional Knapsack, Dijkstra's Shortest Path, Prim's & Kruskal's MST.
     * **Dynamic Programming**: 0/1 Knapsack full matrix construction, Floyd-Warshall ($O(V^3)$), Matrix Chain Multiplication (MCM), Longest Common Subsequence (LCS).
     * **Backtracking**: N-Queens Problem and State-Space Trees.
   * *Why*: If you watch Abdul Bari's videos on DAA, you can easily score 90%+ in the VTU external exam.
2. **Gate Smashers — DAA Playlist**:
   * *Search*: `Gate Smashers NP Complete NP Hard`
   * *What to watch here*: P vs NP, NP-Complete, NP-Hard, Cook's Theorem, and Hamiltonian Circuit reductions.
   * *Why*: Simplifies confusing theoretical computer science concepts into clear bullet points.
