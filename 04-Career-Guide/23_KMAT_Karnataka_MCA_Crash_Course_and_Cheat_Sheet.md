## 🧮 MODULE 1: Quantitative Ability (Math Shortcuts Without Algebra)

*Since you are weak in math, memorizing these 5 basic arithmetic tricks will fetch you 15–20 easy marks!*

### 1. The Super-Fast Percentage Trick
Never use complex fractions. Use the **10% and 1% method**:
* To find **10%** of any number: Move the decimal point **one place left**.
  * Example: 10% of 480 = **48**
* To find **5%**: Take half of 10%. (Half of 48 = **24**)
* To find **20%**: Double the 10%. (2 × 48 = **96**)
* To find **1%**: Move decimal point **two places left**. (1% of 480 = **4.8**)

> **Exam Question Example:**  
> *A laptop costs ₹40,000. It is offered with a 15% discount. What is the discount amount?*  
> - 10% of 40,000 = ₹4,000  
> - 5% of 40,000 = ₹2,000 (half of 4,000)  
> - **Total 15% discount = 4,000 + 2,000 = ₹6,000!** (Solved in 5 seconds without pen!)

---

### 2. Profit & Loss Shortcuts
* **Profit** = Selling Price (SP) − Cost Price (CP)
* **Loss** = Cost Price (CP) − Selling Price (SP)
* **Profit %** = `(Profit / Cost Price) × 100` *(Always calculate on Cost Price!)*

> **Exam Question Example:**  
> *A shopkeeper buys an item for ₹500 and sells it for ₹600. What is his profit percentage?*  
> - Profit = 600 − 500 = ₹100  
> - Profit % = `(100 / 500) × 100 = 1/5 × 100 =` **20%**.

---

### 3. Ratio & Proportion: The "Box Method"
When a total amount is divided in ratio `A : B`:
* Add the ratio parts: `Total Parts = A + B`
* One Part Value = `Total Amount / Total Parts`

> **Exam Question Example:**  
> *₹1,200 is divided between A and B in the ratio 3 : 2. How much does A receive?*  
> - Total parts = 3 + 2 = 5 parts.  
> - Value of 1 part = 1,200 / 5 = ₹240.  
> - A has 3 parts: `3 × 240 =` **₹720**.

---

### 4. Average Formula
* **Average** = `Sum of all items / Number of items`
* **Sum of all items** = `Average × Number of items`

> **Exam Question Example:**  
> *The average age of 4 students is 20 years. When a teacher joins, the average becomes 22 years. What is the teacher's age?*  
> - Total age of 4 students = `4 × 20 = 80 years`  
> - Total age of 5 people (including teacher) = `5 × 22 = 110 years`  
> - Teacher's age = 110 − 80 = **30 years**.

---

### 5. Simple Interest Formula
$$\text{SI} = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$$
* **P** = Principal (original money)  
* **R** = Rate of interest per year  
* **T** = Time in years  

> **Exam Question Example:**  
> *Find Simple Interest on ₹5,000 at 10% per year for 2 years.*  
> - `SI = (5000 × 10 × 2) / 100 = 100,000 / 100 =` **₹1,000**.

## 🧩 MODULE 2: Logical & Abstract Reasoning (Your Highest-Scoring Zone)

*This section requires zero math! It tests common-sense patterns.*

### 1. Alphabet Letter Positions: The `EJOTY` Code
Memorize this single word: **`E - J - O - T - Y`**
* **E** = 5th letter
* **J** = 10th letter
* **O** = 15th letter
* **T** = 20th letter
* **Y** = 25th letter

> **How to use it:**  
> If an exam question asks for letter **R**:  
> You know **T** is 20, so R is two steps before T: `20 − 2 =` **18th letter**!

---

### 2. Coding-Decoding Patterns
Examine what happens to each letter:
* **Forward shift (+1, +2):** `CAT` ➔ `DBU` (C+1=D, A+1=B, T+1=U).
* **Reverse alphabet:** `A` ➔ `Z`, `B` ➔ `Y`, `C` ➔ `X`.

---

### 3. Direction Sense Problems
Always draw a small cross on rough paper:
```text
           NORTH (N)
               ↑
  WEST (W) ←---+---→ EAST (E)
               ↓
           SOUTH (S)
```
* **Golden Rule:**
  * When walking **North**, your right hand is **East**, left hand is **West**.
  * When walking **South**, directions reverse: right hand is **West**, left hand is **East**.

> **Exam Question Example:**  
> *Rohan starts from point A and walks 4 km North, then turns right and walks 3 km. How far is he from point A?*  
> - This forms a right-angled triangle with sides 4 km and 3 km.  
> - Distance = $\sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = \sqrt{16 + 9} = \sqrt{25} =$ **5 km**.

---

### 4. Blood Relations: The Tree Diagram Method
Use simple symbols:
* Males = **`[ + ]`**
* Females = **`[ - ]`**
* Marriage = **`A = B`** (Double line)
* Siblings = **`A — B`** (Single horizontal line)
* Parents & Children = Vertical line (Parents on top, children below)

> **Key Relationships to Remember:**
> * Mother's/Father's brother = **Uncle** (Maternal / Paternal)
> * Mother's/Father's sister = **Aunt**
> * Son of brother/sister = **Nephew**
> * Daughter of brother/sister = **Niece**
> * Uncle/Aunt's children = **Cousin**

---

### 5. Number Series Patterns (Look for These 4 Types)
1. **Constant Addition / Subtraction:** `3, 7, 11, 15, 19` (+4 each time).
2. **Multiplication / Division:** `2, 6, 18, 54, 162` (×3 each time).
3. **Square & Cube Numbers:**
   * Squares: `1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100`
   * Cubes: `1, 8, 27, 64, 125`
4. **Alternating Series:** `2, 10, 4, 20, 6, 30` (Odd terms: 2, 4, 6... Even terms: 10, 20, 30...).

## 📖 MODULE 3: Verbal Ability & English Hacks

### 1. Subject-Verb Agreement (The Most Repeated Error Question)
* **Singular Subject takes Singular Verb** (`is`, `was`, `has`, `runs`).
* **Plural Subject takes Plural Verb** (`are`, `were`, `have`, `run`).

> ⚠️ **The Tricky Rule in KMAT:**  
> When subjects are joined by **"as well as"**, **"along with"**, or **"together with"**, the verb follows the **FIRST subject**!  
> * "The teacher, along with the students, **is** (not are) attending the seminar." *(Because 'teacher' is singular!)*

## 🎯 10 High-Probability KMAT Practice Questions

### Q1 [Reasoning - Series]:
**Find the missing number in the series: 3, 8, 15, 24, 35, ?**  
- **Trick:** Look at differences between numbers:  
  $8 - 3 = 5$  
  $15 - 8 = 7$  
  $24 - 15 = 9$  
  $35 - 24 = 11$  
  Next difference must be $13$!  
- **Answer:** $35 + 13 =$ **48**.

---

### Q2 [Math - Percentages]:
**If the price of petrol increases from ₹80 to ₹100 per liter, find the percentage increase.**  
- **Formula:** `(Increase / Original Price) × 100`  
- Increase = $100 - 80 = 20$  
- Percentage = $(20 / 80) \times 100 = (1/4) \times 100 =$ **25%**.

---

### Q3 [Reasoning - Coding]:
**If 'BOOK' is coded as 'CPPL', how is 'DOOR' coded?**  
- **Pattern:** Each letter shifts by +1 (B+1=C, O+1=P, O+1=P, K+1=L).  
- For DOOR:  
  D + 1 = **E**  
  O + 1 = **P**  
  O + 1 = **P**  
  R + 1 = **S**  
- **Answer:** **EPPS**.

---

### Q4 [Math - Time & Work]:
**A can finish a work in 10 days, and B can finish it in 15 days. How many days will they take working together?**  
- **Fast Formula:** $\frac{A \times B}{A + B}$  
- $\frac{10 \times 15}{10 + 15} = \frac{150}{25} =$ **6 days**!

---

### Q5 [Reasoning - Blood Relation]:
**Pointing to a man, a woman said, "His mother is the only daughter of my mother." How is the woman related to the man?**  
- Break it down from the back:  
  *"My mother's only daughter"* = The woman herself!  
  So, *"His mother is [the woman herself]"*.  
- **Answer:** The woman is his **Mother**.

---

### Q6 [Math - Ratios]:
**Two numbers are in the ratio 4 : 5 and their sum is 180. Find the smaller number.**  
- Total parts = $4 + 5 = 9$ parts.  
- 1 part = $180 / 9 = 20$.  
- Smaller number (4 parts) = $4 \times 20 =$ **80**.

---

### Q7 [Verbal - Sentence Correction]:
**Choose the correct sentence:**  
A) Neither Rohan nor his friends is coming.  
B) Neither Rohan nor his friends are coming.  
- **Rule:** When subjects are joined by "Neither... Nor", the verb agrees with the **closest subject**. Here, "his friends" is plural, so verb must be "are".  
- **Answer:** **B is correct**.

---

### Q8 [Reasoning - Direction]:
**A man walks 6 km South, turns left and walks 8 km. Find the straight-line distance from his starting point.**  
- Forms a 6-8-10 right triangle! $\sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{36 + 64} = \sqrt{100} =$ **10 km**.

---

### Q9 [Math - Averages]:
**The average of 5 numbers is 18. If one number is excluded, their average becomes 16. Find the excluded number.**  
- Sum of 5 numbers = $5 \times 18 = 90$  
- Sum of remaining 4 numbers = $4 \times 16 = 64$  
- Excluded number = $90 - 64 =$ **26**.

---

### Q10 [Verbal - Antonym]:
**What is the opposite (antonym) of "FRUGAL"?**  
A) Thrifty  
B) Extravagant  
C) Honest  
D) Miserly  
- "Frugal" means careful with money. The opposite is someone who wastes money.  
- **Answer:** **B (Extravagant)**.
