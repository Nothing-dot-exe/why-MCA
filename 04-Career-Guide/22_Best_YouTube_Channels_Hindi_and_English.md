## 🎯 How to Learn from YouTube (The Non-Coder Golden Rule)

Before opening any channel, remember this:
> ⚠️ **The Trap:** Watching tutorials on 1.5x speed while lying in bed gives you the *illusion* of learning. You will forget 95% of it within 48 hours.  
> ✅ **The Fix:** **"Type Every Line."** Have YouTube open on one half of your screen, and VS Code / Terminal open on the other half. Every command the instructor types, you type with your own fingers.
