## 1. Executive Summary of MCA Findings on `vtu.ac.in/pg-scheme-syllabus/`

The official VTU Postgraduate Scheme & Syllabus repository hosts curriculum structures, semester syllabi, professional elective syllabi, and official university circulars for MCA under multiple schemes:
1. **2024–2026 Scheme (Recent Scheme - NEP 2020 / NCrF Aligned)**:
   - Complete 1st to 4th Semester Scheme breakdown
   - 1st Semester Detailed Syllabus
   - 2nd Semester Detailed Syllabus
   - 3rd Semester Professional Electives Syllabi (8 distinct tracks including GenAI and Ethical Hacking)
   - Additional Bridge Course Mathematics syllabi (`1MMC110` & `MMAT108`)
2. **2022–2024 Scheme (Last Years Scheme - CBCS 100 Credits)**:
   - Master Scheme Document
   - 1st & 2nd Semester Detailed Syllabus
   - 3rd & 4th Semester Detailed Syllabus
   - Specialized circulars (22MCA331 Blockchain & 22MCA43 Technical Seminar)
3. **Historical MCA Schemes (2020 & Previous Schemes)**:
   - 2020 Scheme & Syllabus (2-Year MCA Transition)
   - Master PG MCA Syllabus & Regulations

All documents listed below have been fetched, verified, and safely archived into this folder.
