## 🔄 GPU Lifecycle

> ### 🔄 Complete GPU Lifecycle
> 
> 1. 🏭 **Fabrication & QA (Month 0):** Fabricated at TSMC on advanced nodes. Stress-tested and binned (5–10% die defect rejection).
> 2. 📦 **Logistics & Deployment (Months 1–2):** Packaged with high-bandwidth memory (HBM), shipped to hyperscalers (Azure, AWS, OCI, Meta).
> 3. ⚡ **Active Training & Inference Life (Years 1–4):** Operated 24/7 at 700W+ thermal load in liquid-cooled server racks.
> 4. 🔄 **Secondary Cloud & Fine-Tuning Market (Years 4–6):** Decommissioned from frontier model clusters; repurposed for inference, enterprise fine-tuning, or sold to secondary markets.
> 5. ♻️ **Decommissioning & Material Reclamation (Years 6+):** Precious metals (gold bonding wires, copper heat spreaders, rare earths) recovered (~80% recyclable material efficiency).
