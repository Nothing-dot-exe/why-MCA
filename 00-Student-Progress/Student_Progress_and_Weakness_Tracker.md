## 📌 1. Learning Philosophy & Teacher-Student System

This document is the **living log of your MCA journey**. Every lesson we complete, every quiz you take, every mistake you make, and every milestone you achieve is recorded here and synced to GitHub.

### The 5-Step Weakness Remediation Framework:
1. **Diagnostic Quiz**: We run a quick 3-to-5 question diagnostic on a topic.
2. **Mistake Categorization**: Did you get it wrong due to:
   - *Conceptual gap* (didn't know the rule/formula)?
   - *Calculation/speed error* (rushed, arithmetic slip)?
   - *Question misinterpretation* (fell into an exam trap)?
3. **Remediation Drill**: Re-teach the shortcut/concept using practical analogies.
4. **Targeted Practice**: 3 immediate similar practice questions to cement the pattern.
5. **Mastery Status Update**: Move the topic from 🔴 **Weak** ➔ 🟡 **In Progress** ➔ 🟢 **Mastered**.
