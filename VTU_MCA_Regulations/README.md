## Essential Examination & Academic Rules (Applicable to Current Students)

### 1. Passing Criteria (CIE vs. SEE)
* **Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE — 50 Marks):**
  * You must score **at least 50% (25 out of 50 marks)** in CIE.
  * ⚠️ **Strict Bar Rule (`Clause 24OMC9.8`):** If a student scores less than 25 marks in CIE, **they are disqualified from sitting for the University Semester End Examination (SEE)** in that subject.
* **Semester End Examination (SEE — 50 Marks scaled from 100):**
  * You must score **at least 40% (40 out of 100 marks)** on the external written exam.
* **Aggregate Requirement:**
  * Total combined $(\text{CIE} + \text{SEE})$ must be **at least 50%** in every subject.
  * Minimum Passing Letter Grade: **`C`** (Grade Point $\ge 5.0$).

### 2. Mandatory Attendance Rule (`Clause 24OMC11.3`)
* **Minimum Required Attendance:** **85%** in each subject.
* **Condonation:** A shortage of up to **10–15% can be condoned** (down to 70–75%) only on documented medical grounds or certified university sports/cultural representations approved by the Vice-Chancellor.
* Students falling below the limit are barred from writing the exam and must re-register for the course.

### 3. Vertical Progression & Backlog Limits (`Clause 24OMC12.3`)
* **Sem 1 $\rightarrow$ Sem 2 (Odd to Even):** Promotion is **automatic**; no credit cutoff exists between 1st and 2nd semester (provided attendance was maintained).
* **1st Year $\rightarrow$ 2nd Year (Progression to Semester 3):**
  * ⚠️ **The 16-Credit Rule:** A student carrying **more than 16 credits of active backlogs (`F` grades)** across 1st and 2nd semesters **will NOT be permitted to enter 3rd semester (Year-Back)**.
  * All backlogs must be within 16 credits to proceed into Year 2.

### 4. Percentage & Degree Division Formula (`Clause 24OMC8.1` & `8.2`)
VTU computes your percentage directly from your Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA):
$$\text{Percentage (\%)} = \text{CGPA} \times 10$$

* **First Class with Distinction (FCD):** $\ge 70\%$ ($\text{CGPA} \ge 7.00$)
* **First Class (FC):** $60\% \text{ to } 69.9\%$ ($\text{CGPA } 6.00 - 6.99$)
* **Second Class (SC):** $50\% \text{ to } 59.9\%$ ($\text{CGPA } 5.00 - 5.99$)
