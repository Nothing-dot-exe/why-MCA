## Course Code: 22MCA43 / Seminar (2 Credits) | Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU)
### Department of MCA | BKIT College, Bhalki

## 📑 Course Objective & VTU Evaluation Scheme

* **Total Credits**: 2 Credits | **CIE Marks**: 100 Marks (or 50 CIE / 50 SEE depending on regulation scheme).
* **Core Objective**: To cultivate literature survey proficiency, critical scientific appraisal, technical writing skills compliant with IEEE/ACM standards, and oral technical dissemination before an academic panel.

---

# SECTION 1: TOPIC SELECTION & RESEARCH RIGOR

### 1.1 Criteria for High-Scoring Seminar Topics
* **Emerging / High-Impact Domains**: Generative AI & Foundation Models, Edge AI & IoT Orchestration, Zero-Trust Architecture & Quantum-Safe Cryptography, Web3/Blockchain Scalability (Layer 2 rollups), Neuromorphic Computing.
* **Literature Base**: The seminar topic must be substantiated by at least **5 to 8 recent peer-reviewed papers** published in Scopus-indexed, IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, Springer, or Elsevier journals/conferences within the last 3 years.
* **Avoid**: Generic, outdated overviews (e.g., "History of the Internet", "Basics of Cloud Computing"). Focus on an analytical comparison or novel methodological paradigm.

---

# SECTION 2: IEEE STANDARD TWO-COLUMN RESEARCH REPORT FORMAT

A technical seminar report must be formatted according to the IEEE Transactions standard template:

```
[ TITLE: Concise, Specific, Descriptive (14pt Bold, Centered) ]
[ Author Name, USN, Department of MCA, BKIT College, Bhalki ]

ABSTRACT
A single paragraph between 150-250 words outlining:
1. Context & Motivation: Why is this research domain vital?
2. Research Gap: What specific limitation or bottleneck exists in current solutions?
3. State of the Art: What recent methodologies are evaluated?
4. Key Findings: Quantitative performance metrics (e.g., latency, accuracy, throughput).
5. Conclusion: Future research trajectory.

Keywords: 5 to 6 terms separated by commas (e.g., Transformers, Edge Computing, Zero-Trust, PyTorch).

I. INTRODUCTION
* Historical background, industry/academic motivation, overview of the technical challenge.
* Summary of major contributions of this seminar paper.

II. BACKGROUND & THEORETICAL FOUNDATIONS
* Architectural primitives, mathematical formulations, protocols, and standard baselines.

III. SYSTEMATIC LITERATURE REVIEW & COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS
* Detailed appraisal of reviewed papers.
* Include a Comparative Synthesis Table:
  +------------------+------+----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+
  | Reference / Year | Core Methodology     | Primary Metrics       | Major Advantage     | Identified Limit    |
  +------------------+------+----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+
  | Vaswani (2017)   | Self-Attention       | BLEU: 28.4            | Parallelizable      | O(N^2) Complexity   |
  | Dao et al. (2022)| FlashAttention       | 3x Speedup            | IO-aware tiling     | Hardware specific   |
  +------------------+------+----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+

IV. ARCHITECTURAL & METHODOLOGICAL DEEP DIVE
* Detailed breakdown of the leading algorithmic or architectural solution.
* Block diagrams, sequence interactions, pseudocode, and mathematical equations.

V. OPEN RESEARCH CHALLENGES & FUTURE OPPORTUNITIES
* Critical analysis of unaddressed bottlenecks (e.g., energy consumption, adversarial robustness, scalability).

VI. CONCLUSION
* Concise wrap-up synthesizing the critical takeaways from the literature survey.

REFERENCES
* Strict IEEE citation style:
  [1] A. Vaswani et al., "Attention is all you need," in Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), vol. 30, pp. 5998–6008, 2017.
```
